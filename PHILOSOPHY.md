# ⓿ Flow Day — Product & Productivity Philosophy

> *"Plan with light brushstrokes; record with honest precision."*

Flow Day is designed around a simple yet profound realization: **rigid micro-scheduling kills momentum, while uncaptured days leave no trace.**

Traditional productivity software often forces users into one of two extremes:
1. **Hyper-prescriptive micromanagement**: Specifying every 15-minute slice of the future in granular detail, which collapses the moment reality deviates from the plan (leading to schedule guilt and overdue task fatigue).
2. **Passive retro-logging**: Merely recording history after it happens, losing the intentionality and focus required to accomplish deep work.

Flow Day bridges this divide through a philosophy of **Directional Intent + Frictionless Reality Capture**.

---

## 1. The Principle of "Light Brushstrokes" (Broad Titles)

### Why Task & Block Titles Should Not Be Too Detailed
When planning a day or defining focus sessions:
- **Time Blocks are intentional arenas**, not rigid prisons. Titles like `Deep Work: Auth Module`, `Morning Synthesis`, or `Client Outreach` establish cognitive boundaries without dictating every breath.
- **Tasks are milestones of intent**, not microscopic step-by-step manuals. A task like `Draft architecture proposal` is actionable and clear. Breaking it down upfront into `Open Google Docs`, `Write Intro`, `Add Diagram 1`, `Fix Typos` creates cognitive friction and perfectionist dread before you even start.

```
❌ Over-Prescriptive: "Refactor lines 40-120 of auth.ts and write 4 test cases for expired JWTs"
✅ Focused Intent:    "Auth token refresh logic"
```

### The Power of Low Cognitive Overhead
- When capturing a task, your working memory should be spent on the work itself, not on engineering the perfect task title.
- Lightweight titles encourage fast capture, effortless rescheduling, and spontaneous action.

## 2. Intent vs. Reality: The Ecosystem of Entry Types

Rather than forcing everything into a "todo checklist", Flow Day separates your day into distinct cognitive layers that work together harmoniously:

```
                  ┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
                  │                 YOUR DAY                      │
                  └───────┬───────────────────────────────┬───────┘
                          │                               │
             ┌────────────▼────────────┐     ┌────────────▼────────────┐
             │         INTENT          │     │         REALITY         │
             │     (What you plan)     │     │    (What happened)      │
             └────────────┬────────────┘     └────────────┬────────────┘
                          │                               │
            ┌─────────────┴────────────┐     ┌────────────┴────────────┐
            │                          │     │                         │
            ▼                          ▼     ▼                         ▼
     [ TIME BLOCKS ]               [ TASKS ] [ LOGS / HABITS ]     [ NOTES ]
   Strategic arenas for          Milestones   Granular truths,    Insights, ideas,
   dedicated focus               of action    bread-crumbs & logs & synthesis
```

### The Spectrum of Entry Types

| Entry Type | Role | Temporal Nature | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`time-block`** | **Container / Arena** | Broad scheduled span | `Deep Work (2:00 PM – 4:30 PM)` |
| **`task`** | **Milestone of Intent** | Point in time or flexible | `Fix cache invalidation bug` |
| **`log`** | **Factual Reality** | Timestamped retroactive record | `Found memory leak in socket connection pool` |
| **`note`** | **Reflection & Idea** | Point-in-time synthesis | `Idea: Use Redis hash sets for user sessions` |
| **`event`** | **External Anchor** | Fixed temporal commitment | `Product sync with Design team` |
| **`habit-log`** | **Consistency Pulse** | Timestamped daily ritual | `Daily Workout / Hydration logged` |

## 3. How Logs Complement Tasks

If task titles are broad and light, where does the detail live? **In the Logs and Notes.**

1. **Tasks define what you set out to do.**
2. **Logs capture what actually unfolded along the way.**

As you work inside a broad task (e.g. `Implement Billing Flow`), unexpected discoveries, micro-milestones, and decisions occur naturally:
- *10:45 AM* — Task active: `Implement Billing Flow`
- *11:15 AM* — `log`: *Stripe webhook handler was rejecting test signatures due to raw body parsing.*
- *11:50 AM* — `note`: *Need to verify European VAT calculation next sprint.*
- *12:10 PM* — Task completed ✅

By offloading the granular details into real-time **logs** and timestamped **notes**, the task title remains clean, while the day's timeline tells the rich, unvarnished story of your actual output.

---

## 4. The 4 Golden Rules of Flow Day

### I. Capture Instantly, Schedule Deliberately
Drop thoughts and tasks into the Day or Lists View without forcing an exact hour. Use the natural language bar (`@today`, `@tomorrow 2pm`) when timing is critical; leave them untethered when it is not.

### II. Respect the 3-Task Horizon
Do not overwhelm today with 25 open items. Star your top 1–3 focus tasks to create visual priority cards. Protect your attention from decision fatigue.

### III. Let Reality Shape the Timeline
If a task took longer or was finished early, Flow Day's 15-minute dynamic spans and time ruler let you adjust without shame. Completed tasks re-anchor to their true completion time, giving you an honest timeline of your life.

### IV. Journal As You Work
Productivity is not just crossing boxes off a list; it is building a record of clarity. Use short logs, habit pulses, and notes throughout the day to preserve context for your future self.
